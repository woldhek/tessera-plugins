# Changelog

## 0.2.0
- Tap a film on the card for its own page: the director, year and length, a short blurb of what it is about, and its
  next showtimes.
- Two keys there: **Seen** puts the film on your seen list, so it leaves the lists; **Trailer** plays its trailer on
  the media player chosen in the Cineville integration's options.
- Needs the Cineville integration 0.2.0 or later (with an older one the page shows only the title and next showtime),
  and the screen allowed to perform Home Assistant actions (its device page in Home Assistant, "Configure").

## 0.1.0
- First version: the films of the Cineville theaters of your city on a tile, with when and where each shows next and
  Cineville's film of the week and tips. Tap for the whole list on a card.
- Needs the Cineville integration for Home Assistant (through HACS, see the README).
