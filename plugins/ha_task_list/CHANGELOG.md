# Changelog

## 0.2.0
- A new look: every task is a row with a round checkbox, and done tasks are greyed out.
- When a task has a due date or a description, it shows under the task: "Today", "Tomorrow", "Overdue" in red.
- The tile shows as many tasks as fit and "3 more" when the list is longer; tap there to open the whole list on a
  card and page through it with the arrows at the bottom.
- New tile option: Show completed tasks (off by default).
- Tap a task on the tile or the card to tick it off or untick it.
